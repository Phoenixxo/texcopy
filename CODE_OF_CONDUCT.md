# Code of Conduct

We want texcopy to be a friendly place to ask questions, report bugs and contribute, whatever your experience, background or identity.

**Be kind and assume good faith.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Give feedback you would be glad to receive. Newcomers' questions are welcome; there are no stupid ones.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes, sharing someone's private information, sexual attention or imagery, or sustained disruption of a discussion.

**Where it applies:** the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and any space where someone represents the project.

**If something goes wrong:** report it privately through GitHub (a private [security advisory](https://github.com/Phoenixxo/texcopy/security/advisories/new), or a message to the maintainer). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and ban people who break these rules.

This is a short statement in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), which is a good longer read.
